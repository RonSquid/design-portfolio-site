World Almanac for Kids

After designing and updating the interiors of this almanac four years in a row, the client requested to totally upgrade the layouts and photos for the 2016 season with a major rethink of the opening chapter, now featuring pop culture, and a new way to organize the categories starting with the TOC and carrying the look through out the book. Over 400 new photos where brainstormed, researched and introduced. All pages designed, retouched and delivered, high rez, and ready for press. The interior typography developed was picked up and used on the cover.

Client: Scholastic

Services: Art Direction, Graphic Design, High Rez Retouching, Final Page Production

Year: 2016
