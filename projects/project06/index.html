Ola Granola

Ola Granola is a pet project designed to develop, manufacture, and market a new brand of exclusive Granola meeting the needs of consumers for a healthy alternative. Most granola has too much sugar and poor quality ingredients. Ola Granola will feature organic ingredients with no sugar, an option to request maple syrup will be available separately and in very low amounts. A gluten free option is also available. A prototype label and packaging system is in the works with an e-commerce website and small batch production to follow. The working name “Ola” means “wave” in Spanish.

Client: Food Packaging

Services: Branding
